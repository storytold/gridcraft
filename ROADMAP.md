# GridCraft roadmap

**Stage: alpha** · next: beta, ~25 points of "ready for real work" and ~160–240 h away

> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10-11 · **Last updated:** 2026-10-22 · **Change:** trivial (progress log: formula hardening, #2163/#2162; previously minor: Page Setup paper sizes: JIS B4/B5/B6, the Japanese postcards and envelopes, and A6; long flat operator chains up to Excel's formula limit) · **Target:** Microsoft Excel (Microsoft 365)

GridCraft aims at full Microsoft Excel parity, and at being better in places: faster, open (XLSX
is the native format), fully scriptable by agents (MCP, control channel, CLI), and on the web.
This page is the summary. The detail is in:

- [`docs/target-app-parity.md`](docs/target-app-parity.md): the authoritative assessment, method and weights
- [`docs/gaps.md`](docs/gaps.md): every known shortfall, ranked; the work list
- [`docs/roadmap.md`](docs/roadmap.md): milestones, current focus, what's next
- [`docs/parity-checklist.md`](docs/parity-checklist.md): generated by `cargo xtask parity` (ribbon commands)
- Area checklists: [functions](docs/function-parity.md) · [charts](docs/chart-parity.md) ·
  [file formats](docs/file-format-parity.md) · [UI](docs/ui-parity.md) ·
  [hardware](docs/hardware-parity.md) · [localization](docs/localization-parity.md)
- [`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md): how it is built

Each tier is a subset of the one above, so its hours are too. Calibrated as in [Effort and
calibration](docs/target-app-parity.md#effort-and-calibration) (~6–10 h per readiness point for the remaining, verification-heavy
work; single features measured from PRs #66, #100, #101). Parallelism: the full and mainstream
work splits ~65–70% across 4–6 agents by area; the essentials work is mostly small fixes plus one
long `.xls` track, so about half parallelizes (≈30–50 wall-clock hours with three agents).

Why alpha: all six core workflows in the [alpha gate](docs/roadmap.md#alpha-gate) pass end to end on macOS with save and reopen, and the core workflow works end to end (open an XLSX, enter formulas and data, format, sort
and filter, chart, pivot, print to PDF, save XLSX), but users still hit blocking gaps on real
files: 100 MB workbooks don't open (#175), there's no `.xls` (#121), Arabic and Devanagari text
isn't shaped (Chinese text still reported in #142), charts lose their type and options on round trip, and
nobody has yet confirmed that Excel opens our output without a repair prompt across a real
corpus. Beta needs those closed (see [beta gates](docs/roadmap.md#beta-gates)).

The 2026-10-07 numbers (catalog 89%, "feature depth ~65%", "~215 h to full parity") were
re-measured. Catalog and function counts held or rose; the depth and hours moved because of new
evidence: 82 issues filed by users and contributors in the repo's first five days, and scope the
old estimate left out (localization, legacy `.xls`, Power Query and the data model,
collaboration, mobile, AI).

## Languages

The ribbon is translated into Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian
and German (266–268 labels each, ~12% of ~2,200 strings; merged 10-10 in #31, #104, #82, German
added 10-11), the
interface follows the system language, and CJK, Thai and Hebrew text falls back to system fonts
(#38). Japanese (10-17) and Brazilian Portuguese also translate the dialog titles and labels,
message boxes, Backstage/File-view items, template names and native menus: 943 strings each, ~43%.
The remaining dialogs, menus and messages are English; formulas and number formats are en-US only.
Localized German formulas and numbers are in PR #163. Detail: [localization-parity.md](docs/localization-parity.md).

## Upcoming

Ranked; estimates in Opus 5.5 agent-hours. Detail and the full beta-gate list in
[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

   correctness, chart kind round-trip, CJK font fallback, macOS Finder open, German and Chinese
   UI). Review and merge: 8–15 h.
2. **XLSX fidelity on a real-world corpus**, with Excel opening our output without repair: 25–35 h.
3. **Large workbooks and recalc speed**: 100 MB XLSX, 1M rows, multi-threaded recalc: 40–60 h.
4. **Charts depth**: kinds and options round-trip, axes, trendlines, error bars: 25–35 h.
5. **Text in every script** (CJK, Thai, Arabic, Devanagari) in cells and PDF: 12–20 h.

7. **Localization**: extend the catalog past the ribbon, then the twelve languages: 100–165 h.
