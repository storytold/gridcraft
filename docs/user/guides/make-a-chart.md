Select your data, including the header row, open the **Insert** tab and pick a chart type. The chart appears on the sheet.

## Insert a chart

1. Select the cells you want to plot, with their headers. If you click a single cell inside a table of data, GridCraft uses the whole block of data around it.
2. Click the **Insert** tab.
3. Pick a chart:
   - Click **Recommended Charts**. The **Insert Chart** window shows chart types that suit your data on the **Recommended Charts** tab, and every type on the **All Charts** tab, with a preview of each. Pick one and click **OK**.
   - Or click one of the six small chart buttons and choose a type from its menu. Hover over a button to see its name: **Column or Bar**, **Line or Area**, **Pie or Doughnut**, **Scatter or Bubble**, **Statistic** or **Hierarchy & more**.

The chart is placed two columns to the right of your data. If there is nothing usable in the selection, GridCraft says "Select data for the chart first."

If the top-left cell of your data is empty and the first row holds headers, GridCraft uses the first column as the category labels, even when it holds numbers such as years.

## Change the chart

Click the chart. A **Chart Design** tab appears. It has:

| Button                | What it does                                                         |
|-----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Add Chart Element** | Legend position (bottom, right, top or none), data labels, gridlines |
| **Switch Row/Column** | Plots rows as series instead of columns, or the other way round      |
| **Select Data**       | Changes the data range, removes series and sets the axis labels      |
| **Change Chart Type** | Switches to another type, such as Pie, Line or Scatter               |
| **Format Pane**       | Opens the Format Chart pane                                          |
| **Chart Title**       | Sets the title and the horizontal and vertical axis titles           |
| **Delete Chart**      | Removes the chart                                                    |

To resize a selected chart, drag its corner handle. To delete it, you can also press Delete.
